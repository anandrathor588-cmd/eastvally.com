East Velly Premium Fashion E-Commerce

Act as a senior UI/UX designer, professional full-stack developer, and e-commerce conversion specialist. Build a complete, production-quality fashion e-commerce website for a clothing brand named EAST VELLY.

BRAND IDENTITY:
Brand name: East Velly
Industry: Fashion and clothing
Style: Premium, modern, minimalist, elegant, youthful, editorial.
Visual inspiration: Zara, H&M, and contemporary luxury fashion brands, without copying their logos or exact designs.
Color palette: black, white, warm beige, and subtle charcoal.
Typography: bold modern sans-serif headings paired with clean readable body text.

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS:
Create a polished, visually impressive storefront with generous whitespace, large fashion photography, sophisticated layouts, smooth subtle animations, elegant hover states, and consistent spacing. The design must work beautifully on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

PAGES AND SECTIONS:

  1. Home page with a promotional announcement bar, responsive navigation, EAST VELLY logo, search, account, wishlist and shopping bag icons.
  2. Hero section with a high-quality fashion campaign image, headline 'STYLE THAT SPEAKS.', supporting copy, and Shop Now button.
  3. Shop by category: Men, Women, T-Shirts, Shirts, Hoodies, Trousers, and New Arrivals. Use only categories relevant to the actual inventory.
  4. Trending Products and Best Sellers product grids with product images, names, prices in INR, available colors, discount labels where applicable, wishlist controls, and quick-add buttons.
  5. New Collection editorial banner and seasonal collection section.
  6. Brand story section introducing East Velly as a modern fashion destination.
  7. Customer reviews section using clearly identified sample content until genuine reviews are provided.
  8. Newsletter subscription section, Instagram-style gallery, and comprehensive footer with contact information, FAQs, shipping, returns, privacy policy, and terms.
  9. Shop page with functional category filters, sorting, search, price filters, and product cards.
  10. Product detail pages with image gallery, size and color selection, size guide, quantity selector, product details, shipping and return information, and Add to Cart / Buy Now actions.
  11. Shopping cart with quantity updates, remove-item controls, subtotal, shipping calculation where configured, and checkout button.
  12. Checkout flow with customer details, delivery address, order summary, and supported payment methods.
  13. Contact, About Us, FAQ, Shipping & Returns, Privacy Policy, and Terms pages.

FUNCTIONALITY:
Build real, working interactions rather than a static mockup. Use reusable components, clean maintainable code, proper form validation, accessible labels, responsive navigation, loading and empty states, and clear error messages. Persist the cart appropriately. Product search, filters, variant selection, and cart operations must work. Connect checkout to a legitimate supported payment provider only when configured. Never simulate a successful real payment. Do not invent business contact details, reviews, stock availability, discounts, or policies.

TECHNICAL QUALITY:
Use the platform's supported modern frontend stack. If coding from scratch, prefer Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and reusable accessible components. Use a proper product data model and backend/database integration where supported. Include SEO-friendly titles and descriptions, semantic HTML, optimized images, lazy loading, good performance, and basic security best practices. Use environment variables for secrets and never expose API keys. If a backend or payment integration is unavailable, clearly identify what must be configured instead of pretending it works.

CONTENT AND IMAGERY:
Use high-quality fashion photography featuring clothing, textures, outfits, and editorial campaigns. Keep imagery cohesive and premium. Use realistic sample product data clearly marked as demo data, with prices in Indian rupees. Never use copyrighted brand logos or misrepresent demo products as actual stock.

FINAL GOAL:
Deliver a complete, professional, premium-looking East Velly clothing e-commerce website that is easy to navigate, builds customer trust, showcases products beautifully, and is ready for real products and business integrations once configured. Do not stop at a landing page or static design. Generate all supported pages and working components.
